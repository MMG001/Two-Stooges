The dart area

Electronic dart boards in Fridley

Our dart area has a row of electronic boards, high-tops for your crew and a big screen showing the game. It's right next to the billiard room, so your group can switch between pool and darts all night.

  • Electronic dart boards
  • High-top seating right by the boards
  • Food and drinks from the Two Stooges menu delivered to your table
  • Darts and flights in our Pro Shop
