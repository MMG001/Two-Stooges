Our story

About Two Stooges Sports Bar: Fridley since 1988

Two Stooges Sports Bar & Grill is a sports bar, billiards hall and darts room at 7178 University Ave NE in Fridley, Minnesota, in the Twin Cities north metro. Open since 1988, it has 43 pool tables, electronic dart boards, 34 TV screens and 16 beers on tap. Almost four decades in, we're still serving the coldest beer and juiciest burgers around.

How it started

The history of Two Stooges

What began as a neighborhood billiard room became one of the best-known pool and darts destinations in the Twin Cities and Minnesota, with a full sports bar and kitchen to match.

  1. 1988

    University Billiards opens

    University Billiards opens its doors in April 1988 with 17 pool tables and a snack bar.

  2. 1990s

    Room to grow

    The room grows to 56 pool tables and a full-service American restaurant.

  3. 1998

    Billiard Street Café

    A new name for a growing destination. You can still spot the sign out front.

  4. 2006

    Two Stooges is born

    A full sports bar joins the billiard room, and the business becomes Two Stooges Sports Bar & Grill.

  5. Today

    Still racking 'em up

    43 tables, darts, 30 HD TVs plus 4 monster screens, 16 taps, and the same family feel.

The sports bar

A Fridley sports bar with a seat at the center

Find us

Visit Two Stooges Sports Bar in Fridley, MN

We're on University Avenue NE (Highway 47) in Fridley, just north of Minneapolis, serving the Twin Cities within about 30 miles, from Minneapolis and St. Paul to Columbia Heights, Spring Lake Park, New Brighton, Coon Rapids, Blaine and the rest of the north metro. Walk-ins are always welcome. Planning ahead? Reserve space for a group.
