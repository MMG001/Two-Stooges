Why join

Benefits of joining a Two Stooges league

More than just a game night. Here's what you get when you join a league in Fridley.

Meet your people

League night is the easiest way to meet other pool and dart players in Fridley and the north metro.

Compete for cash & bragging rights

Play for standings, prizes and cash, and get ready for our FargoRate tournaments with money added.

Every skill level

Leagues are handicapped, so newer players and sharks can play together, and every match is competitive.

Get better every week

Regular match play against new opponents sharpens your game faster than practice alone, and it builds your FargoRate.

A standing night out

Same night, same crew, every week. It's a built-in reason to get out of the house.

Play on tournament tables

Pool leagues are played on our 9 ft Gabriel's tables with new cloth and rails, in a room named a top ten billiard room in North America.

Do I need a team to join a league?

No. Solo players are welcome. Sign up and we'll help place you on a team or league night.

What skill level do I need?

Any. Our leagues are handicapped, so beginners and experienced players can compete on even terms.

Which leagues can I join?

Straight Pool on Tuesdays at 11 AM, 10-Ball on Thursdays at 11 AM, APA, UPL and in-house 8-ball pool leagues, plus dart leagues every day of the week.

Can I sign up by phone?

Yes. Call (763) 574-1399 and a team member will sign you up.
