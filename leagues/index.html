Pool & dart leagues

Pool & dart leagues at Two Stooges Sports Bar

Leagues at Two Stooges are a great way to meet other pool and dart players in Fridley and across the Twin Cities, and sharpen your skills. There's a league for every skill level on our 43 tables and dart boards.

More leagues

IBA (M8), APA, UPL & dart leagues in the Twin Cities

IBA (M8)

We host IBA (M8) pool leagues. Visit the IBA website.

APA & UPL

We host APA and UPL handicapped pool leagues, open to all skill levels. Visit APA or UPL.

In-house 8-Ball

Our own handicapped 8-ball league, a great first league for newer players.

Dart leagues

Leagues every day of the week, plus custom leagues for women, couples and teams. See the dart area.

Looking for more competition? Check the upcoming pool tournaments: weekly 8-ball and 9-ball plus FargoRate shootouts.

Ready to join a league?

Sign up online in a couple of minutes. Solo players are welcome, and we'll help you find a team. Check league-night drink specials, the bar and grill menu and our league FAQ, or book your end-of-season party.
