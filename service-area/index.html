The 20-mile circle

One sports bar for the whole metro

Two Stooges has one location, at 7178 University Ave NE in Fridley, just north of Minneapolis on Highway 47. Come in from anywhere in the metro for 43 pool tables, electronic dart boards and a full bar and grill menu.

  • 76 communities in 5 counties within about 20 miles
  • Open every day, 11 AM to midnight
  • Parking lot right outside the front doors
  • Pool and dart leagues and pool tournaments open to players from any city

Service area questions

More answers in the full Two Stooges FAQ.

How far is Two Stooges from Minneapolis and St. Paul?

Two Stooges is in Fridley, about 8 miles from the center of Minneapolis and about 14 miles from the center of St. Paul, measured in a straight line. We're on University Avenue NE (Highway 47). Get the address, hours and a map.

Which counties are within 20 miles of Two Stooges?

Communities in Anoka, Hennepin, Ramsey, Washington and Dakota counties sit within about 20 miles of the bar. Every one is listed above, grouped by county.

My city isn't on the list. Can I still come?

Yes. Everyone is welcome at Two Stooges. The list only shows the communities within about 20 miles of Fridley.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is a parking lot right outside the front doors on University Ave NE. Get directions.

Do I need a reservation?

No. Walk-ins are always welcome. For a group, reserve space for a private party.
