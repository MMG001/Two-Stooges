Billiards at Two Stooges Sports Bar in Fridley

Two Stooges has been named a top ten billiard room in North America, and City Pages voted it the best place to shoot pool. There are 43 Gabriel's and Diamond tables in 9 ft and 7 ft, with room to play around every one.

How to rent a table

Renting a pool table at Two Stooges

  1. Stop at the billiard counter. The attendant will set you up with a table and a set of balls.
  2. Play as long as you like and order from the bar and grill menu right at your table.
  3. Bring the balls back to the counter and settle up.
