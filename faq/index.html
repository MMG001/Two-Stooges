Visiting Two Stooges

Where is Two Stooges Sports Bar located?

Two Stooges Sports Bar & Grill is at 7178 University Ave NE, Fridley, MN 55432, on University Avenue (Highway 47) just north of Minneapolis. Get directions and parking info.

What are the hours at Two Stooges?

We're open every day from 11 AM to 12 AM (midnight). Happy hour runs Monday to Thursday 2–6 PM and Friday to Sunday 11 AM–7 PM. See today's specials.

Does Two Stooges have outdoor seating?

Yes. Two Stooges has two patios.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is a parking lot right outside the front doors on University Ave NE. Get directions.

Can I watch NFL games at Two Stooges?

Yes. We carry NFL Sunday Ticket and the DirecTV sports channels on 30 HD TVs and 4 monster screens. More about the sports bar.

Pool, darts & the Pro Shop

How many pool tables does Two Stooges have?

Two Stooges has 43 pool tables, 9 ft and 7 ft Gabriel's and Diamond tables on Simonis 860 cloth. Learn about our billiard room.

How do I rent a pool table?

Stop at the billiard counter and the attendant will set you up with a table and a set of balls. Play as long as you like, then bring the balls back and settle up. Daily pool specials run 11 AM–5 PM.

How many dart boards are there?

Two Stooges has 6 electronic dart boards with high-top seating right next to them. See the dart area.

Is there a pro shop?

Yes. The Pro Shop carries cues, cases, chalk, gloves, darts and accessories, and we do cue repair on site. Visit the Pro Shop.

Leagues & tournaments

Does Two Stooges have pool and dart leagues?

Yes. We run a Straight Pool League on Tuesdays at 11 AM, a 10-Ball League on Thursdays at 11 AM, APA, UPL and in-house 8-ball leagues, and dart leagues every day. Sign up for a league.

Do I need a team to join a league?

No. Solo players are welcome, and we will help place you on a team or league night. Sign up for a league.

What skill level do I need?

Any. Our leagues are handicapped, so beginners and experienced players can compete on even terms.

Does Two Stooges host pool tournaments?

Yes. We host FargoRate 8-ball tournaments and bar box shootouts with money added. See upcoming tournaments.

Food & drink

What food does Two Stooges serve?

Classic American bar and grill food: Stoogetizers, wings, burgers, sandwiches and wraps, pizza, entrées, soup and salad. See the full menu.

When is happy hour?

Monday to Thursday 2–6 PM, and Friday to Sunday 11 AM–7 PM, with daily food specials too. See every special.

How many beers are on tap?

Two Stooges pours 16 beers on tap, ice-cold from our iced cobra taps.

Parties & contact

Can I host a private party at Two Stooges?

Yes. Two Stooges hosts birthdays, office parties, draft parties, banquets and celebrations of life, with custom menus, pool tables and darts. Request a party date.

How do I book a private party?

Send your date, guest count and occasion with the event request form, or call (763) 574-1399.

How do I contact Two Stooges?

Call (763) 574-1399, email info@twostooges.com, or use the contact form.
