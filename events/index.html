499 & Under Fargo Rated 8-Ball Bar Box Shootout

An 8-ball bar box shootout for players rated 499 and under on FargoRate. Only the first 64 players who sign up get in, so call or text early to grab your spot. New to FargoRate? Our weekly pool leagues are a great way to build your rating.

Entry fee$40 · money added
FormatDouble elimination
Race4/3 · alt break
FieldFirst 64 players
Check-in closes11:00 AM
Starts11:30 AM

Doors open at 10:00 AM. Players must have 150 robustness in FargoRate, or entry is at the tournament director's discretion.
